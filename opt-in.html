Messaging Opt-in

Smart Fitout · Udupi, Karnataka

This page explains how Smart Fitout may contact customers after they request a service or voluntarily subscribe to communications.

What you may receive

  • Booking confirmations and updates
  • Quotation and invoice notifications
  • Appointment and service-status updates
  • Customer-support messages
  • Optional promotional offers, only where applicable consent has been provided

How consent works

By actively selecting the consent checkbox on a Smart Fitout form and submitting your details, you agree that Smart Fitout may contact you using the communication channels described in that form, including SMS/RCS, WhatsApp, email and/or phone.

Opting out

You can stop optional promotional messages at any time by using the unsubscribe/opt-out instructions in the message or by contacting Smart Fitout at +91 9606963170. You may still receive essential service-related communications when they are necessary to handle a booking or service you requested.

Privacy

Your contact information is handled according to our Privacy Policy. Please do not use this page to submit passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
