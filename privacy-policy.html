Last updated: September 2, 2026

Smart Fitout respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and booking forms, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

When you request a service, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, service selected, area/location, preferred date and time, and work details. We may also receive information you voluntarily provide when contacting us by phone, WhatsApp, email or other communication channels.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, process service requests, arrange appointments, prepare quotations or invoices, provide customer support, send service-related notifications, prevent misuse and improve our website and services.

3. Messaging and communications

If you opt in, we may send service-related or, where separately permitted, promotional communications by SMS/RCS, WhatsApp, email or phone. You can opt out of optional promotional messages at any time. Service-related communications may still be necessary to complete a booking or provide requested service.

4. Sharing

We may share necessary information with service professionals, technology providers, hosting/database providers and messaging providers when required to deliver the requested service or operate the website. We do not intend to sell your personal information.

5. Third-party services

The website uses third-party services, including Supabase for application/database functionality and messaging or communication providers when enabled. Those providers may process information according to their own policies and contractual requirements.

6. Data security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for bookings, customer support, business records, legal obligations and dispute resolution, after which it may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to correct inaccurate contact information or request that optional marketing communications stop. Some records may need to be retained where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

9. Children

Our services and website are not directed specifically to children. Please do not submit personal information on behalf of a child unless you are authorized to do so.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
